# Frozen particle optics: historical comparison and crossover validation

Date: 2026-10-27. C3 + Probe Corrector, STEM Nanoprobe Normal, static
Finite-Field Optics. Backend-only work. No installed excitation, source
distribution, dimension, position, aperture, detector insertion, or propagation
formula was fitted or changed. New source-count runs regenerate the complete
source; no pruning, weight replacement, or current normalization is used to
hide numerical or physical losses.

The user clarified the aberration condition during this validation: **disable
additional aberration actions while retaining effects naturally generated by
the electromagnetic fields**. This is the frozen unified executor's condition,
not an achromatic first-order replacement. The added probe/image/intrinsic
aberration lists are empty, empirical chromatic correction and equivalent image
lenses are disabled, and all gun/condenser/objective/diffraction stigmator and
corrector quadrupole controls are zero. Nonzero HP1/HP2 physical hexapole fields
remain active. The stored legacy objective Cs of 1.2 mm is not executed as an
additional kick by the unified path. Actual energy dependence and field-derived
nonlinear effects remain measurable; the 0.3-eV source spread is retained.
An independently constructed production plan (without integration) confirms
zero additional spherical/thin-lens/rotation/steering arrays, empty posed
spherical/intrinsic-aberration/thin-action collections, and 40 uniquely owned
field contributions. A2 coarse controls the actual HP fields and is not an
added A2 polynomial to erase. The source file's older comment about the
high-order round-lens field never being selected is stale: the executed
unified runtime selects radial order 3. This audit follows runtime ownership,
not that outdated comment.

## What the historical 30 mrad result meant

The saved 2026-10-14 result really achieved **alpha95 approximately 30 mrad**
with 3,000 particles under the historical `custom` executor. It did not
measure a physical pupil edge or establish particle-count convergence.
The historical 0.05/0.025-mm repeats gave alpha95 29.999987647/29.999987656
mrad, RMS radius approximately 0.1389 nm, and 2,661/3,000 transmitted
particles (8.87 microampere). The sampled maximum angle was 32.8303 mrad;
it is also not a mechanical pupil edge.

The installed unified working point was fitted near alpha95 10 mrad.
The newer user requirement is a **30-mrad physical aperture edge**, with
alpha95 reported independently. These are three distinct comparisons:
historical alpha95 30, current alpha95 about 10, and the requested edge 30.
The separately executed nominal-energy reference-source mechanical pupil
maps to **53.538-54.115 mrad**, while the emitted source strongly underfills
the limiting C2 Aperture. See the
[physical-pupil report](FINITE_FIELD_PUPIL_ACCEPTANCE_2026-10-27.md).

### The older model already had thickness

Historical `custom` integrates finite axial Gaussian round-lens profiles and
finite quadrupole/hexapole fields using canonical paraxial RK4 propagation.
It also applies calibrated spherical-aberration kicks. It was **not** today's
separate thin-lens `ideal_thin` mode. The recorded HP2/HP1 effective lengths
remain 6/10 mm; this comparison did not introduce their thickness.

Unified transport evaluates a shared spatial potential and forward Lorentz
trajectory. Its retained round-lens expansion is

\[
A_\phi=\frac{rB_0}{2}-\frac{r^3B_0''}{16},\quad
B_z=B_0-\frac{r^2B_0''}{4},\quad
B_r=-\frac{rB_0'}{2}+\frac{r^3B_0'''}{16}.
\]

Equivalent Cs/Cc kicks and image maps are disabled where this executor owns
the interaction. An old empirical-Cs correction fit therefore need not cancel
the field-derived nonlinear response. The experiment identifies a substantial
focus/nonlinear-model mismatch; it does **not** isolate one coefficient as the
sole cause. The current field is still a bounded near-axis engineering model,
not an OEM field map or a general pole/material solution.

The historical/current controls differ notably at C2 (23.2256/13.8990%), C3
(9.2080/15.6882%) and both hexapole strengths (current values about 10.3 times
the recorded historical values). The current geometry and the old complete
snapshot were compared field by field. Changed pre-existing numeric geometry
fields chiefly concern the downstream shared Descan/Image-Diffraction package,
not a new incident-column lens spacing. Different configuration hashes alone
were not treated as proof of a changed incident geometry.

## Complete-source count and step checks

The complete source-count extension and comparison gates are saved in
`outputs/extended-optical-validation-20261027/count-convergence.json` and
the matching Markdown/PNG. Every input signature is checked after excluding
only numerical source count and maximum column step. Emission mean energy
**0.3 eV** and energy-spread FWHM **0.3 eV** remain separate, unchanged inputs.
The current bounded non-Gaussian sampler implements the spread through
`standard deviation * 2.354820045`: it is an RMS-equivalent FWHM, not a
new measurement of the distribution's half-maximum width. This assumption is
recorded without changing either source parameter.

The sampler restandardizes emission energy with population size. Thus higher
N requires a freshly generated complete source, not appending rays to an older
checkpoint. Real gun exit energies, flight times and original current weights
are handed to production column transport. Memory-bounded batches use the same
full-population integration mesh and preserve particle order.

The existing 193-particle 0.05-to-0.025-mm incident check changes alpha95 by
about 2.58e-8 mrad, RMS radius by -0.0000203 nm, and local radial focus by
-0.028665 nm. This small step sensitivity does not repair the much larger
source-count sensitivity or certify high-N step convergence.

Numerical gates retain the earlier engineering budgets: 0.25% for alpha95,
2% for RMS/D95/current, and 0.25 nm absolute local radial-focus change.
The relative angular scale is the nonzero finer-count result, not 30 mrad.
Two successive count refinements must pass. Current is an observed quantity,
with no imposed current floor or ceiling.

### Refined specimen and downstream limitations

The earlier coarse 1599.2-mm 2D-waist label does not survive nanometre-scale
refinement. The radial minimum lies about **4.2866 nm after specimen entrance**,
with RMS radius 0.170238 nm. At that point the symmetric transverse
position/slope covariance has opposite-sign eigenvalues of about
plus/minus 2.931e-14 m rad: one direction still converges while another
diverges. This remains unresolved 2D focus, not an accepted common focus.

The 3,073-particle capture contains a different near-specimen radial candidate,
about 3.26335 nm upstream of entrance. Safe continuation from its saved exact
200-nm-upstream checkpoint resolved that candidate in 8.624 seconds, without
rerunning the source or column. Executed 0.25-nm observations confirm a radial
minimum at 1599.1999942366474 mm, RMS radius 0.300318650 nm, with directional
covariance eigenvalues approximately plus/minus 3.66817e-15 m rad. The strict
common-focus condition is still unresolved. The original entrance is reproduced
to 3.30e-18 m in position and 1.75e-15 in slope, with identical survival.
These count-dependent directional residuals must not be fitted as physical A1
before source-count convergence is established.

The corresponding 12,289-particle local continuation completed in **15.056 s**
from its saved 200-nm-upstream state, without repeating the gun or incident
column. Its 0.25-nm observation grid covers the radial minimum. The lowest
executed grid point is 2.750000 nm upstream of entrance, RMS radius
0.284651483 nm and alpha95 9.842057978 mrad. Interpolation brackets a radial
candidate at 1599.1999947549461 mm; it is not an accepted common 2D focus.
At the lowest executed point the symmetric covariance eigenvalues are
-3.076849e-15 and +2.930728e-15 m rad, again opposite in sign. Entrance
reproduction differs by at most 3.33e-18 m in position, 1.77e-15 in slope,
1.49e-23 s in clock and 5.82e-11 eV in energy. All original particles and
10-microampere current are retained. This additional local propagation does
not change the failed source-count convergence result.

The downstream calculation retains the installed diffraction-projector
settings and physical detector interception, but uses a **vacuum/unscattered
reference**. It is not a Si diffraction or scanning image. The main screen is
retracted; 2714.15 mm is only a geometric observation plane. All 193 particles
and 10 microampere reach it; the inserted HAADF does not intercept this beam.

On 2,243 shared exact planes, halving the downstream maximum step from
0.05 to 0.025 mm changes alpha95 by at most 2.238e-7 mrad and RMS radius by
at most 0.005071 nm. The largest individual position-component change is
0.011204 nm. Current, survival and kinetic-energy changes are zero. The finer
run also adds local waist observations; its root-position changes must not be
attributed solely to integration step. Retained upstream error and downstream
source-count convergence remain outside this step comparison.
