## Field and scan measurements

The magnetic input to the particle integrator combines the impressed field of the installed windings and the induced field of the magnetized bodies in a single nonlinear three-dimensional solution. The gun uses the same magnetic solution in addition to its electrostatic potential. All 94 ferromagnetic volumes in the 153-part installed assembly were matched to the model. They comprise 82 ARMCO pure-iron regions and 12 nickel–iron pole pins. The joint mesh has 1,102,557 nodes and 6,841,876 tetrahedra before the zero-volume degeneracies are removed. The winding-load interpolation was compared against direct field evaluations at 2,048 points per channel. The largest error relative to the corresponding peak was 0.318%. [Installed winding and material audit](evidence/material-relay/field-null-trial/field-audit.json)

For static tracking, all four AC windings have zero current. During calibration, each of the four winding responses includes the incremental magnetization of the complete biased circuit. Independent positive and negative horizontal and vertical commands measure the two-dimensional centroid displacement and tilt. Both singular values of the tilt response must remain finite at the common minimum of the two position responses. The records also give the actual ampere change in each winding per command.

The illumination-side front is measured by point tilts in the isolated Mini Condenser and physical series objective, with the installed iron in place. The upper and lower objective winding packs each have 580 turns and share one supply. The reference plane is the specimen entrance. The reported focal condition uses the angular transfer in canonical Larmor coordinates. The mechanical scan-angle response at the specimen is recorded separately. The all-windings transfer provides an independent check of the effect of overlapping upstream fields.
