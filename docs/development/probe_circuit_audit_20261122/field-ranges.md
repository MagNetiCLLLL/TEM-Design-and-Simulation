# Field ranges in the assembled probe corrector

Column z increases towards the specimen. Round lenses use the on-axis longitudinal field. HP2, HP1 and the two HPol channels use the magnitude of the complex transverse sextupole coefficient at a radius of 1.2 mm. Each interval is relative to the same drive's own maximum. The curves are sampled every 0.5 mm. Weak fringe fields continue outside the listed bands. ## Fields present at the winding centers

The third column is the signed total axial field generated by this corrector at the chosen currents. The last column measures how strongly another excited channel acts at that location, as a percentage of its own peak response to a current change. A dash means that each of the other channels falls below 10% of its own peak. The percentage is independent of the signed total axial field. Steerers and trims at zero drive are included as receiving locations.
