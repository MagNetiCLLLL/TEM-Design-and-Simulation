# Probe corrector implementation and particle acceptance plan

## Scope and starting point

This plan combines the repair and acceptance guide v2.0 (§§6–12) and the CEOS implementation guide v1.0 (§§2–14, 16), both dated 2026-11-01. Their code audit refers to revision `23768e904333`. The current working tree also contains subsequent research diagnostics and measurements. Preserve that distinction in the execution record.

Start from the [accepted A5 working point](probe_stage12_20261101/inputs/portable-probe.temwp). Its sample entrance and target coincide at 1599.1999975 mm. The saved measurement gives 29.777 mrad current-weighted alpha95, 10.000 µA transmitted, 2.330 µA in a 50 pm radius about the commanded axis, 2.502 nm centroid-based D95 and 1.710 nm RMS radius. The representative plane of the combined Mini Condenser and Upper Objective waist is 0.1 nm upstream. These are regression references with numerical uncertainty, not universal limits. [Accepted evidence](probe_channels_20261101/accepted-a5.json)

Use the static unified-field classical particle solver and the identical 2,000 positive-current original particles for all production comparisons. Freeze IDs, emission phase space, weights, energies, clocks, source denominator, 300 kV illumination, the 100 µm C2 opening and the Normal branch. Keep the established 29.7–30.3 mrad angle band and reference transmission. A separate zero-current diagnostic pencil measures the optical response. The user selects the next aberration from the image.

## Acceptance decisions

- For each lens interval, report a cumulative geometric coefficient only at a resolved two-dimensional crossover with a valid pencil fit. Show separate statuses for an absent waist, unresolved topology and an inadmissible coefficient fit. Retain the combined-field interval when a mechanical boundary splits the final waist.
- Select the target observable and its minimum meaningful improvement before solving. A sixfold tail, for example, needs both A5 and whole-field/annular sixth moments, including absolute outer-tail current. For an upper limit, require the measured value plus its uncertainty to fit within the declared budget. The previous 5 pm collateral edge-displacement limit belongs to the A5 experiment; review its applicability before reuse.
- Verify C2–C3, C3–ADL, TL22–TL21 and the AC coil interval against the Normal optical branch. Record all real losses and retain independent through-focus extrema, including boundary or unresolved minima. A smaller spot from clipping or an improved inner annulus alone does not establish improved probe performance.
- Deliver a versioned baseline, channel cards and matrix, candidate/rollback receipts, through-focus data, particle current image, and a gate ledger. Use descriptive gate names because the two guides number their gates differently. Mark any stronger source-count convergence claim as unassessed under the fixed-population study.
- Coherent or partially coherent PSF, Strehl, diffraction and in-material atomic imaging belong to a separate future scope. Specific CEOS model equivalence additionally needs independently verified winding, unit-current field and measured channel-response data for the relevant instrument and operating mode.
