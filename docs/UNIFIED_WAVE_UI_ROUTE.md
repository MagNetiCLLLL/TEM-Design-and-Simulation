# Unified coherent execution in the UI

The `unified` mode connects the existing actual tip/gun producer to
`UnifiedPotentialWaveAdapter`. The adapter consumes the same immutable
`UnifiedFieldRuntime` field and geometry capture as the new particle route.
It runs the admitted scalar electromagnetic carrier operator and the
finite physical bore/aperture executor. The historical finite-field mode
keys keep their historical propagation behavior.

No fitted exit pupil or synthetic Gaussian replaces an executed gun wave.
Source probabilities, incoherent mode identities, analytical carriers,
longitudinal action and time reference are retained. The existing explicitly
budgeted source-mode omission remains recorded. The explicit numerical request
`column_kinetic_order` selects H2, H4, H6 or H8 (default H4). H6/H8 use the
declared higher-order real-scalar Strang split; the remaining differential
operator retains all noncommuting products. The operator policy is part of
the executed identity and cannot change during continuation.

`column_support_projection` is disabled by default. When explicitly enabled,
the existing grid limits remain relative to each original source-mode weight
`w`: the fixed per-reference budgets are `epsilon*sqrt(w)` and `delta*w`.
Neither absorption nor continuation reissues a budget, and a zero weight or
zero limit enables no projection. Every accepted projection retains its
receipt; aggregate L2 is the square root of summed squared per-mode bounds,
and probabilities are summed. Shared material prefixes are counted once and
cannot fund descendant projections. These receipts bound projections only;
different FFT windows still need independent propagated-field convergence.
The enabled executor explicitly records `step_boundaries_and_retry`: an
admitted canonical output may be compacted before reserving the following
residual's larger scratch memory. Its full boundary, spectrum and physical
domain are checked before this projection, and the projected field is checked
again. This scheduling policy is part of the executor identity.
Physical stops reduce absolute weights. Each
mode/history carries one cumulative 0.01 rad kinetic truncation allowance;
material energy changes inherit only its remaining allowance. The exponential
series allowance is allocated by distance over the fixed captured interval.
Neither allowance is an experimental accuracy certificate.

After an intervening recording device, the continued geometry receipt's
`entry_weight_per_reference` is an effective geometry budget with that
device's received probability subtracted. It is not a replacement for the
original gun/source weight. `external_recording_losses` retains each received
and transmitted weight separately, and the source and detector receipts remain
available. Geometry's own wall-loss balance uses this effective entry once;
reconstruct the earlier entry by adding the separately received probability,
without counting that reception a second time as a geometry-wall loss.

Structural admission happens before source execution or source-cache lookup.
Unsupported mapped/FEM magnetic wave providers, unqualified electric bases,
and temporal discontinuities are rejected explicitly.
Fields, physical boundaries and the canonical chart run on the CPU. The
complete residual exponential may execute on a CUDA device under the requested
backend policy; each exponential substep retains its boundary and spectral
checks and returns a host wave. Records distinguish the requested residual
policy from actual backends. A permitted resource fallback starts again from
the unchanged host input; `Require GPU` is not weakened. Source and atomic
material stages retain their existing backend policy. In
particular, the continuous cold-FEG electrostatic tail is retained by the
common field snapshot and is **not** approximated as zero. The native scalar
potential changes local energy and the same Hermitian kinetic operator.
Each executed energy history has an additional cumulative 1e-6 scalar
forward-branch amplitude allowance. Native scans use the explicit retarded
reference-wavefront model and a separate cumulative 0.01 rad added timing/work
phase allowance. These are conditional post-gun model bounds, not a proof of
the gun's transverse time distribution or a full time-domain wavepacket.

An exact native `grounded_downstream` electrostatic continuation is a narrower
supported case: its whole column interval must be after the solved boundary.
The actual incoming kinetic energy supplies the constant-potential reference,
and a mathematical zero-field domain guard preserves the electric provider's
strict radial domain. Native compact gun-alignment fields are omitted only
when their complete support is disjoint from the captured column interval.
These exact reductions, the full original field digest and the separately
derived wave snapshot digest are stored. No amplitude threshold removes a
field tail.

Static finite axial specimens use the existing atomistic elastic/inelastic operators
with the new field and geometry propagation between their actual material
actions. Each energy/phonon history retains its own geometry and kinetic
ledger through downstream propagation and real recording stops. Non-vacuum
holder supports, tilted material slabs, and dynamic material scattering remain
explicitly unsupported. Continuous static acceleration retains its original
energy reference; a proved physical collision starts a new energy reference
at the same face while inheriting only the remaining error allowances.
Carrier/grid, physical-boundary and material sampling gates remain active.
The probability-current readout evaluates A from the executed potential
snapshot at its exact SI plane; it never substitutes the legacy on-axis Bz
gauge. Stable added electric longitudinal phase and dynamic temporal action
are included once in the actual complex envelope consumed by phase plates.
Only the original entrance-energy free longitudinal carrier stays factored
out. The separate total axial-action record must not be applied a second
time. Phase remains per coherent mode, not a phase of an incoherent mixture.

The regressions include an actual low-NA tip/gun producer followed by a
declared vacuum continuation, and the same executed source through the
captured physical C1 field at 1% and 2% excitation. The latter compares raw
phase on common physical coordinates and independently halves the axial
step. These are bounded source/handoff/response fixtures, not a claim that
the entire default source-to-detector microscope is qualified.
Actual finite Si multislice, multiple energies and frozen-phonon branches
exercise the material handoff separately. Same-plane identity checks,
fresh stored continuation and recording-stop losses test cache and lineage
preservation. No default optical settings are calibrated by these tests.

Electric regressions separately compare a two-way one-dimensional
Klein-Gordon slab, positive-branch flux, energy/action/time quadrature,
and the full operator square including mixed electric differential terms.
Retarded native-coil regressions compare the actual complex wave against an
independent time-dependent affine-force Gaussian, including offset-coil
induced work and the temporal principal-action term. The phase comparison
uses the ordinary full-wave/readout interface without a fitted piston or
an external corrective phase. See `UNIFIED_DYNAMIC_EXECUTION.md` for scope.


Canonical spatial-domain growth is an explicit numerical policy. The public
`WaveGridNumerics.angular_domain_padding` defaults to `doubling`, preserving
historical callers. Unified vacuum-column propagation explicitly selects
`minimum_full_band`: retain all executed cells, reserve the full represented
spectral displacement, and recheck the complete output boundary. Material
field substeps explicitly retain `doubling` reserve before the next specimen
phase and bandwidth operation. This stage choice is part of executor/cache
identity and each material history's `material_domain_policy` receipt. It does
not alter the previously captured specimen ROI, potential array or frozen
phonon realization. Old material results remain readable, but an executed
material history without this receipt must be replayed from its entrance;
continuation must not invent which domain policy produced it.

Advanced coherent controls expose the residual exponential method (Taylor or
Chebyshev) and maximum Chebyshev group separately from kinetic order, axial
step, physical optics and device memory. Editing either invalidates existing
observations; mode changes retain the draft while disabling unified-only
controls in compatibility modes. Default requests continue to select Taylor.
The selected method and group are included in executor and cache identity,
including same-plane readout and material continuation.
