## Minimal shared B-rep construction

1. **Use existing sections as the authority.** `magnetic_circuits.radial_profile_mm` supplies any explicit `(z_offset, inner_radius, outer_radius)` section; convert its outer edge plus reversed inner edge to one closed `(z, r)` wire. Otherwise use the exact `_pole_section` contour from `part_model_3d.py`. Its reversed C1/C2 face convention must remain unchanged.
2. **Separate physical children.** Revolve each non-self-intersecting pole, winding, yoke or housing wire by 360 degrees about local Z. Winding is a homogeneous copper/insulation pack at this fidelity, not guessed individual turns. Use the saved `material_class` and evidence note. Lens optical parents are assembly/reference nodes, not overlapping solid material.
3. **Keep objective interruptions.** Consume `magnetic_geometry.objective_layer_intervals_mm` for the actual upper/lower coil and yoke intervals. Form distinct solids at those intervals. A bounding cylinder spanning both is an allocation envelope and cannot replace them.
4. **Preserve all frames.** Sections use selected-module local Z in millimetres. Apply the existing part/parent rigid placement once, and only once, after construction; do not derive placement from a field centre or from optical display scale.
5. **Use one geometry definition twice.** A dependency-free analytic solid specification should drive both the existing mesh preview and optional STEP/OCC export. Do not independently regenerate the STEP dimensions with separate formulas. Validate saved explicit 3D features before applying the same holes/slots/cuts to the solid and preview.
6. **Report unresolved detail.** Current 0.38-times-length pole-nose fallback is reconstruction. `pole_root_fillet_radius_range_mm` is a range note, not one manufacturable radius; leave existing sharp shoulders until an explicit radius is selected and saved. Cooling paths, coil terminals, turns, exact magnetic alloy and hidden cartridge interfaces are not established by present TOML or the matched public drawings.

The first executable CAD stage can preserve every current material surface without inventing new dimensions. A later source-backed internal-detail stage needs explicit saved parameters and evidence before introducing spacers, cooling channels or a modified magnetic connection. Geometry refinement alone must not silently change the analytical field model or its saved settings.

## Projector pole face lands: saved dimensions now consumed (2026-10-21)

The eight DL/IL/P1/P2 poles in `NoEnergyFilter.toml` and the shared `projector_stack.toml` have long saved `pole_face_land_axial_thickness_mm = 3`. Previously the renderer and CAD ignored that value and tapered the outer contour all the way to the pole face. The revised common contour reserves the final 3 mm at the saved tip radius for a cylindrical land. The nose length is interpreted as the **complete shoulder-to-face interval including the land**: the taper runs from `length - nose` at the outer radius to `length - land` at the tip radius, followed by the cylindrical tip up to the unchanged face. This is an explicit engineering interpretation of existing dimensions, not newly obtained manufacturer geometry.

With the saved OD52 mm and tip OD40 mm, this removes exactly `396π = 1244.070690821558 mm³` per pole from the former straight taper. It adds no material outside the previous solid. Axial endpoints, bore ID21.5 mm, outer envelope, pole-face area, inter-pole gap, optical references, excitation and all calibration parameters remain unchanged. The saved nose lengths remain 12 mm (DL/IL), 13 mm (P1) and 14 mm (P2). Missing land fields retain the old zero-land contour. An explicitly supplied land must be finite and satisfy `0 < land < nose`; impossible dimensions are rejected before editor history changes. Explicit replacement geometry or an explicit radial profile supersedes this native recipe, and restoring the recipe validates it again.

The legacy `pole_cone_angle_to_axis_deg = 63` is not consistent with these saved radii and nose lengths. It remains a read-only reference; it does not drive a second contour. Fillet ranges likewise remain unselected reference ranges. The current dimensions and chosen nose/land convention should not be described as verified OEM pole machining.

Native preview and CAD use the same section.
