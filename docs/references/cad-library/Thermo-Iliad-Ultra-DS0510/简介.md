# Iliad Ultra (S)TEM for materials science

Primary original: [Thermo-Iliad-Ultra-DS0510](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/MSD/Datasheets/iliad-ultra-datasheet-ds0510.pdf).

Evidence: Page 2 Zebra schematic and specification table; page 3 Ultra-X aggregate acceptance.

- Indirect detection with five independent 2048-pixel strips, 14 micrometre pixels and a 256 by 2048 alignment area.
- Camera deflection selects spectral strips.

Limits:

- No measured alignment placement, package size, strip height/pitch, scintillator thickness or optical coupling topology.
- Does not infer individual EDS sensor area or distance.

The original PDF and unchanged page renders are listed with hashes in [source.json](source.json). Engineering adoption is documented in [cad-detectors.md](../../cad-detectors.md).
