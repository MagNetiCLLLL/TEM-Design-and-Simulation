# Residual aberrations of hexapole-type Cs-correctors

Source: [CEOS GmbH](https://www.ceos-gmbh.de/de/produkte/residualsCEXCOR); Authors: P. Hartel, H. Mueller, S. Uhlemann, M. Haider; Date: webpage undated.

[Original webpage](original.html), with file SHA and status in [source.json](source.json).

Location: Sections Tuning Residual Aberrations and Experiment: Tuning fifth-order spherical aberration.

## Supported content

- An intermediate image at the TL21/TL22 symmetry plane is used to suppress D4.
- TL1 intermediate magnification/HP strength can tune C3; C5 trades off against off-axis coma.

## Unsupported claims

- No calibration of this instrument's currents, positions or mechanical dimensions.
