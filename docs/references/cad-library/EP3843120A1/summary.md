[Original public source](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3843120A1/en). Checked locations: [0022]-[0029] (PDF p3); Fig.1 (PDF p7).

## Supported information

- Another diffraction holder with an integrated goniometer specifies 200-300 mm total length, 2-3 mm grids, and vacuum-isolation structures.

## Unsupported extrapolations

- Its architecture differs; total length and specimen-to-seal distances cannot be transplanted as Titan/Spectra OEM dimensions.
- Used only for exterior scale comparison, not current numerical defaults.
