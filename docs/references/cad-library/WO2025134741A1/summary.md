# Sample holder tip and sample holder

[Original public source](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025134741A1/en). Location checked: [0036]-[0039] (original Japanese PDF p10, printed p8).

## Supported statements

- [0036] mentions the narrow shaft from FEI (now Thermo Fisher) and explicitly specifies a phi 7 mm holder shaft.
- [0037] describes a beta-tilt mechanism perpendicular to the holder axis.

## Unsupported extrapolations

- The patent concerns a special retractable/biased tip; it supports only the shaft diameter of a compatible structural family, not the full dimensions of an ordinary Titan/Spectra OEM holder.
- It does not justify adding electrodes, retraction drives or in situ accessories absent from ordinary holders.
