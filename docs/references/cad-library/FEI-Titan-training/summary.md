Reference ID: `FEI-Titan-training`; category: Titan mini condenser and illumination system.

Organization/applicant in original record: FEI; public university host Oregon State University.
Date: null.
Location: Module 1 pp. 1, 4; Figs. 1, 5; PDF pages 3, 6.

## Supported content

- Manufacturer training diagrams explain the optical relationship between C1/C2/C3, the mini condenser and the upper objective.
- Optical switching of the mini condenser involves opposite current directions; it must not be interpreted as the coil current always being zero.

## Content not established by this source

- The optical diagram is not a manufacturing cross-section and cannot provide internal dimensions by scaling.
- The local PDF is an image scan without a text layer.
