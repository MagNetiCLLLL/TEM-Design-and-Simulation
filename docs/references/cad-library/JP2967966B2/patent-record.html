に切断部を作り、誘導電流を遮断する方法について主に
説明したが、誘導電流を低減することができれば同等の

成する部材を抵抗の高い素材で製作するようにしてもよ
い。As described above, the method of cutting off the induced current by making a cut portion in the electric closed loop circuit around the coil has been mainly described. However, if the induced current can be reduced, the same operation and effect can be achieved. The member forming the electric closed loop may be made of a material having high resistance.

【００１９】[0019]

【発明の効果】電子レンズのコイル周辺部の閉ループ回
路の誘導電流を極小化し、当該回路の時定数を小さくし
短時間で誘導電流を消滅させたことにより、電子レンズ
の磁束密度の変化時における応答特性は著しく改善さ
れ、誘導電流についての考慮を払わない場合に比較して
応答速度が１桁以上速くなる。According to the present invention, the induced current of the closed loop circuit around the coil of the electronic lens is minimized, the time constant of the circuit is reduced, and the induced current disappears in a short time. The response characteristics are remarkably improved, and the response speed is increased by one digit or more compared to a case where the consideration of the induced current is not taken into account.
