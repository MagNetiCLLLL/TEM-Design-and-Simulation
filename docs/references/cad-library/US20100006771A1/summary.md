# Speciman holder and speciman holder movement device

[Original public source](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20100006771A1/en). Checked locations: Fig. 1 (PDF p2); [0010] (PDF p4); [0097] (PDF p10-11).

## Supported information

- A conventional side-entry shaft enters the column through a guide sleeve and airlock seals; Fig.1 labels the shaft, external handle, and two sealing rings.
- [0010]/[0097] give a 6-10 mm shaft/front-end diameter range; X runs along the shaft, and alpha rotates about X.

## Unsupported extrapolations

- This is not a Titan/Spectra manufacturing drawing and supplies no shaft length, handle diameter, or thin-tip thickness.
- The range does not mean a 7 mm round shaft can enter this instrument's 5.4 mm pole-piece gap.
