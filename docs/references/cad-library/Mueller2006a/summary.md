# Advancing the Hexapole Cs-Corrector for the Scanning Transmission Electron Microscope

Source ID: `Mueller2006a`; category: CEOS CESCOR optics and physical hexapoles.

Original: [public source](https://www.ceos-gmbh.de/en/produkte/stemkorrektoren/dcor-ascor/Mueller2006a.pdf).
Authors/inventors: Heiko Müller; Stephan Uhlemann; Peter Hartel; Maximilian Haider.
Institution/applicant in the original record: Microscopy Society of America; author copy hosted by CEOS GmbH.
Date: "2006".
Location: Figs. 2-3, journal pp. 443-444; PDF pages 2-3.

## Supported information

- The paper explicitly lists DPH/DP/BTlt/BSh alignment functions in the corrector.
- Text on p443 explicitly states that HP1/HP2 each have six ferromagnetic pole pins made of nickel–iron alloy.
- Fig. 3 is a front-view photograph of the physical assembly, not a mechanical section; pole pieces, coils, and yoke are visible, and the caption explicitly states that a liner tube is present but not shown.
- It establishes the physical hexapole structural family of the main CESCOR HP; absence of polecount in TOML must not be interpreted as a bare tube with no internals.

## Information not established by this source

- The optical diagram does not provide DPH winding-host details.
- Its 8 mm bore, 30 mm pole length, and 152 mm yoke OD belong to the 2006 design, not current project dimensions; the two must not be conflated as identical.
